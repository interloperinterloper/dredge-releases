dredge.

Save video and audio from links to your computer. Free, local, private.

About

Paste a link from almost any video or music site and dredge saves it as a file: the video as an mp4 (best quality, or capped at 1080p, 720p or 480p) or just the audio as MP3, M4A or WAV. Everything runs on your own computer, so nothing is uploaded anywhere and there are no accounts, limits or ads. It keeps a list of your downloads with a button that jumps to the file, and it refreshes its downloader by itself, because sites change often and break old versions.

Works with

  • YouTube
  • SoundCloud
  • TikTok
  • Instagram
  • Vimeo
  • X / Twitter
  • Twitch
  • Reddit
  • Facebook
  • Bandcamp
  • Mixcloud
  • Dailymotion
  • and many more sites

Logos belong to their owners, and dredge is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these services. Private or login-only posts can't be saved.

How it works

  1. Run the installer. It sets everything up the first time you open it.
  2. Paste a link and choose video or audio.
  3. Click Download. The file lands in Downloads\dredge.

Needs

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • An internet connection, for setup, updates and downloading
  • About 500 MB of disk space
